# First Six Weeks

**Weeks 1–2: Prepare for Drift Club.** After introductions and getting my bearings, I would ask my team and the Drift Club team about their biggest concerns, known risks, preferred mitigations, and possible roll back strategy. As I am new to the system, I would listen before prescribing solutions and learn about the platform's capabilities and ownership areas. With the game, product, and operations leads, I would agree on monitoring and escalation contacts, decision points for launch and the first days after release. We would focus the remaining pre-launch time on increasing test coverage and reporting for Drift Club's paths, clarifying the USD/EUR catalog mismatch,or why `vip.lounge` is listed but not grantable, and how queued grants should appear as pending.

**Weeks 3–6: Broaden the scope.** After launch, I would meet the other game teams with the same questions and build a prioritized backlog with them. Iron Siege would be an early focus because its support history has a comparatively high share of purchase related reports. I would work with the teams to verify the evidence before treating reports as defects, then partner with the platform and storefront teams on high-risk purchase coverage and shared reporting. Although the support tickets do not identify a confirmed refund defect, I would investigate refunds early because the handover says they follow a common path across all games. I would also expand UI verification and test coverage so that the storefront user experience is monitored, not just backend outcomes.

**Building trust.** I would be transparent about what I found, what remains unverified, and the limits of current coverage. I favor clear ownership and close collaboration. QA should not gatekeep releases, our role is to provide accurate risk information so teams can make informed decisions together. By being constructive and approaching difficult conversations openly and with good intentions, I would aim to create a safe environment where people can share concerns and ask each other for support.
